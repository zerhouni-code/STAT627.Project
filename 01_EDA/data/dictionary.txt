MBG_WIDTH,"The horizontal measurement of a tree's foliage and branches, usually defined as the average distance between the outermost driplines on opposite sides of the tree. It serves as a key indicator of tree vigor, competitive capacity, and canopy closure in forestry and ecological studies. Measurements are in feet and were derived from 2020 lidar data.","Department of Transportation, Urban Forestry Administration"
MBG_LENGTH,"The vertical measurement of a tree from the top (highest live foliage) down to the base of the live crown, typically defined as the lowest, non-epicormic, live branch. It represents the active, photosynthetic portion of the tree, indicating its health, vigor, and capacity for growth. Measurements are in feet and were derived from 2020 lidar data.","Department of Transportation, Urban Forestry Administration"
MBG_ORIENTATION,"The directional, three-dimensional arrangement of a tree's branches and foliage, often indicating asymmetric growth due to light competition, wind (flagging), or slope. It is quantified by measuring the horizontal spread or projection area from the stem in specific cardinal directions, highlighting which side holds the greatest volume or vigor.","Department of Transportation, Urban Forestry Administration"
MAX_CROWN_HEIGHT,"The vertical distance from the base of the lowest live, sustainable branch to the absolute highest point (apex) of a tree. It represents the total vertical extent of the photosynthetic canopy, excluding dead lower branches, and serves as an indicator of tree vigor, health, and age. Measurements are in feet and were derived from 2020 lidar data.","Department of Transportation, Urban Forestry Administration"
MAX_MEAN,"The average vertical distance from the base of the live crown (lowest live branches) to the top of the tree, representing the average vertical extent of the foliage across a sample. It indicates tree vigor, shading capability, and competition for light, with larger crowns often signifying better health. Measurements are in feet and were derived from 2020 lidar data.","Department of Transportation, Urban Forestry Administration"
MIN_CROWN_BASE,"The vertical distance from the ground to the lowest continuous, living branch or foliage whorl on the main stem. It signifies the starting point of the tree's productive canopy, excluding epicormic sprouts and lower, dying branches. Measurements are in feet and were derived from 2020 lidar data.","Department of Transportation, Urban Forestry Administration"
DTM_MEAN,"Refers to the specific point where the base of a tree’s trunk (the root collar) meets the surrounding ground surface. This measurement serves as the baseline for determining total tree height, typically measured from the ground up to the highest living branch. Measurements are in feet and were derived from 2020 lidar data.","Department of Transportation, Urban Forestry Administration"
PERIM,"The outermost boundary of a tree's branches and foliage projected onto the ground. It represents the total horizontal width or extent of the canopy, commonly measured by identifying the widest diameter and a second diameter at 90 degrees. Measurements are in feet and were derived from 2020 lidar data.","Department of Transportation, Urban Forestry Administration"
CROWN_AREA,"The upper, leafy portion of a tree comprising branches, foliage, and reproductive parts extending from the main trunk. As the primary site for photosynthesis, it acts as the interface for light, water, and gas exchange, with its spread often measured to the outer dripline. Measurements are in square feet and were derived from 2020 lidar data.","Department of Transportation, Urban Forestry Administration"
